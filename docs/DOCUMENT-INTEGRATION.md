## Candidate selection in the final deck

Slide 4 scores each candidate from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Complexity is inverse: 5 means few systems/simple integration, while 1 means many systems or complex modelling.

| Candidate | Relevance | Feasibility | Data availability | Inverse complexity |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| Traffic congestion and emissions | 5 | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| ZTL / historic-centre access | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Heatwaves and urban heat | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Large events and tourism peaks | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| Flooding and extreme rainfall | 4 | 2 | 3 | 2 |

The final slide does not state criterion weights or a weighted total. Earlier working-document weights must not be presented as final-slide evidence. These candidate scores are separate from synthetic intervention scores and do not demonstrate data readiness or numerical benefits. Flooding remains a possible selective extension with data, skills and a funded owner; it is not a fourth active GreenPulse workflow.

## Evidence and prototype boundaries

Real Leuven geography supplies spatial context only. Asset counts describe the supplied reference municipality, not a verified inventory of Leuven's systems. Sensor placements, operational examples and scenarios are synthetic. No identifiable licence-plate records are displayed.

The Evidence drawer now follows the four cases in slide 3. It records the reviewed final presentation and concise background metadata without inventing external benchmark citations. Method references are supporting material, not evidence that a municipal integration or calibrated model is already operational.

The browser prototype demonstrates human authority through explicit role confirmations. Those controls are not municipal identity authentication. Decisions and logs belong to the running demonstration session; refresh/reset clears them. Durable access control, retention and municipal audit storage remain implementation work rather than hidden capabilities of this repository.
