## Implemented contract

- Exactly three workflows: **Daily Traffic Management**, **ZTL / Historic-Centre Access Management**, **Heatwave Preparedness**.
- One shared municipal decision capability; each workflow exposes purpose, current evidence, data gaps, actors, decision path, human approval, KPI impact and supported/unsupported claims.
- Exactly seven KPIs, with readiness states and missing inputs shown as **No data**.
- The explicit 18.0 → 16.2 minute example is illustrative, never measured or achieved.
- CO₂ remains estimated; NO₂/PM2.5 require separate measured evidence. No automatic causal air-quality or emissions claim follows from traffic improvement.
- Traffic requires Mobility Department approval and separate authorised execution. Municipal Police leads ZTL operational/legal action. Civil Protection approves / activates heat preparedness.
- Approvals, rejections, modifications and executions are decision-log events. No AI action executes autonomously.
- Architecture retains the five specified layers and the progressive-validation label.
- Roadmap is **0–6 Discover & Prepare / 6–18 Integrate & Pilot / 18–36 Extend Selectively**.
