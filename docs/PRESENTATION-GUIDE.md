# GreenPulse: 15-minute presentation guide

Present the method, assumptions and proposed outcomes using a few concrete examples. The application supports the reviewed 18-slide **CURRENT FINAL version-3.pptx**; it does not replace its logic or establish real municipal integrations.

| Time | Focus | Demonstrate |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **0:00–1:00** | Positioning | Target-state prototype; real Leuven geography, synthetic operational scenario. Three workflows share one decision capability. |
| **1:00–4:00** | Daily Traffic | Existing cameras/loops/controllers/ITS/GIS; counts and queue patterns at monitored locations. Explain timestamp-linkage validation before the K1 corridor pilot. |
| **4:00–6:00** | One KPI example | 18.0 → 16.2 minutes gives a 10% illustrative change. Read the visible note; neither value is a measured or achieved municipal result. |
| **6:00–8:00** | Accountability | Mobility Department reviews and approves; a separate authorised operator executes. Show Decision Log evidence, including modification or rejection. AI never executes. |
| **8:00–10:00** | ZTL and Heat boundaries | ZTL entries are not automatically violations; Police/legal approval remains mandatory. Heat uses official forecasts and indicative territorial priorities, with Civil Protection approval. |
| **10:00–12:00** | KPI readiness | K4/K7 are pilot-ready through logging; K1/K6 are conditional pilots; K2/K3/K5 require target data. Missing input is No data. |
| **12:00–14:00** | Architecture and roadmap | Five layers. 0–6 Discover & Prepare, 6–18 Integrate & Pilot, 18–36 Extend Selectively. Zoom into one validation gate. |
| **14:00–15:00** | Evidence and limitations | Final source authority, supporting methodology, repository/demo links and the remaining validation requirements. |

The final benchmark assessment is **Trikala / Venice / Pavia / Leuven** (slide 3). Slide 4 explains unweighted 1–5 candidate scores and selects the three operating workflows. Use these examples to explain transferability and selection; do not confuse candidate scores with the synthetic intervention scores in Digital Twin.

## A reliable live narrative

1. Open Overview and identify the persistent prototype/synthetic disclaimer.
2. Use Presentation Demo to follow the evidence and decision narrative. The guided controls may navigate; they must not approve or execute on behalf of the presenter.
3. In Daily Traffic, explain source coverage and why observed counts/queues do not yet establish corridor travel time or network-wide congestion.
4. In KPI Framework, use K1 as the worked example. Show readiness, formula, missing real inputs and the illustrative calculation note.
5. Demonstrate explicit human approval and separate execution. Record a rejection or modification if useful; inspect its Decision Log entry.
6. Briefly inspect ZTL Access and Heat Preparedness to explain their distinct authorities and claim boundaries.
7. End with Architecture and Governance & Roadmap. Data validation enables capability; a proposed delivery date alone does not.

## Phrases to use accurately

- “This is synthetic demonstration data on real Leuven geography.”
- “Counts and queue patterns are supported at monitored locations; corridor travel time requires validated linkage.”
- “These values demonstrate the KPI calculation. We will establish the real baseline during pilot preparation.”
- “Estimated CO₂ is separate from measured NO₂ and PM2.5.”
- “Recorded ZTL entries are not automatically violations.”
- “The heat-priority map is indicative/modelled; it is not a live local heat-exposure feed.”
- “K7 measures complete recording, not decision quality.”
